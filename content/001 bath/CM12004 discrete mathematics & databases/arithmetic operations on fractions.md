#fractions
### addition and multiplication
we define addition of two fractions as:$$\displaystyle {\frac {a}{b}}+{\frac {c}{d}}={\frac {ad+bc}{bd}}.$$
we define multiplication as:$$\displaystyle {\frac {a}{b}}\cdot {\frac {c}{d}}={\frac {ac}{bd}}.$$
for two rational numbers $p,q\in \mathbb{Q}$, where $p$ and $q$ are two [[equivalence class]]es in $\mathbb{Q}$, their sum $p+q$ and product $pq$ are defined as follows:
1. choose an arbitrary fraction $\frac{x_1}{y_1}\in p$ and $\frac{x_2}{y_2}\in q$.
2. $p+q$ is defined as the rational number ([[equivalence class]]) containing the fraction $\frac{x_1}{y_1}+\frac{x_2}{y_2}$.
3. $pq$ is defined as the rational number ([[equivalence class]]) containing the fraction $\frac{x_1}{y_1}\cdot\frac{x_2}{y_2}$.

#### subtraction and division
subtraction and division can be defined in similar ways to addition and multiplication.
$$\displaystyle {\frac {a}{b}}-{\frac {c}{d}}={\frac {ad-bc}{bd}}.$$
$${\displaystyle {\frac {\,{\dfrac {a}{b}}\,}{\dfrac {c}{d}}}={\frac {ad}{bc}}.}$$
## well defined
arithmetic operations over $\mathbb{Q}$ are **well defined**, that is the result of an arithmetic operation does not depend on the particular choice of arbitrary fractions $\frac{x_1}{y_1}$ and $\frac{x_2}{y_2}$.
