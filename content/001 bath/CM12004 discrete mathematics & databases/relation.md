#databases #sets 
a [[relation]] between two sets $A,B$ is a subset of $A\times B$ where $x\in A$ is **related to** $y\in B$ if the pair $(x,y)\in R$, where $R$ is the set for a specific [[relation]].

if the two sets of the relation are equivalent ($A=B$), we can say that the relation **is on $A$**, and $R\subset A\times A$.
