## [[relatively prime]] integers 
two non-zero integers $a,b\in\mathbb{Z}$ are called *[[relatively prime]]* if their greatest common divisor (GCD) is **1**.
for example, $7$ and $10$ are [[relatively prime]], as the $GCD(7,10)=1$.
however, 6 and 10 are **not** [[relatively prime]], as $GCD(6,10)=2\ne 1$.
**theorem.** $a,b\in\mathbb{Z}$ are [[relatively prime]] **if and only if** $\exists u,v\in\mathbb{Z}\mid au+bv=1$.
an easier way to think about this theorem is by rearranging: $au-1=bv$. for example, $(7\times 3)-1=20=2\times10$
