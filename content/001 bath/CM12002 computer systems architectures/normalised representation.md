### normalised representation
any number can be represented in terms of two quantities, namely a signed *mantissa* $m$ and a signed *exponent* $x$:
$$\text{Number: }\pm m\times R^{\pm x}$$
where $R$ is the base or *radix* of the system (i.e: 10 in decimal).
the representation is *normalised* if:
$$1\le|m|<R$$
